[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That license covers hobby projects, personal
study, and charitable, educational, public research and government organisations. It does **not**
cover use by a company or in a product that generates revenue.

This document describes the separate license that does.

## Who needs one

You need a commercial license if any of the following is true:

- You use the library in software your company sells, licenses, or runs as a service.
- You use it internally at a for-profit company, including on tooling that never ships.
- You redistribute it as part of a library or application you license to others.

You do **not** need one for anything the Noncommercial license already permits.

customers would each need their own license from the licensor.
